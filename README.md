A free synthesizer inspired by the Oberheim OB-X, maintained by discoDSP since 2014.

## OB-Xd 2 is now part of Retromulator

Starting with Retromulator 2.2.0, OB-Xd 2 ships as a core inside [Retromulator](https://www.discodsp.com/retromulator/), listed under Analog. It keeps its own panel, presets, MIDI Learn and MIDI maps, and adds improved DSP and new functions over the last standalone release.

[![Get Retromulator](https://img.shields.io/badge/Get-Retromulator-000000?style=for-the-badge)](https://www.discodsp.com/retromulator/)

New OB-Xd 2 updates will come through Retromulator releases.

## Features

- Micro random detuning per voice
- Multimode filter: blendable LP, notch/BP and HP in 12 dB mode, 4 to 1 pole in 24 dB mode
- 8 voices with per-voice filter, glide, envelope and pan variation
- Saw, square, triangle and noise oscillators with sync, cross modulation and pitch envelope
- 2 ADSR envelopes and an LFO
- MIDI Learn

## OB-Xd 3

[OB-Xd 3](https://www.discodsp.com/obxd/) is our commercial synth with a synthesis engine rewritten from scratch, developed by discoDSP together with original OB-Xd author [2Dat](https://github.com/2DaT/Obxd). See the [comparison](https://www.discodsp.com/obxd/comparison/).

## History

- 2012 to 2014: original development by [2Dat](https://github.com/2DaT/Obxd)
- 2014 to 2026: development and maintenance by discoDSP
- December 2022: private use license granted by the original author
- 2023 to 2024: OB-Xd 3 developed by discoDSP and 2Dat
- November 2026: OB-Xd 2 moves into Retromulator 2.2.0
